## What this is

A self-contained Three.js WebGL scene of rising determination particles (520k across two systems), a glowing ascending spine, energy helices, orbiting orbs, pulsing core, and cinematic lighting. Hyper-realistic material response (MeshPhysicalMaterial with clearcoat, metalness, roughness, emissive, transmission, iridescence, ACESFilmic tone mapping, soft shadows, UnrealBloomPass + OutputPass) with animated particle streams that refuse to fall and respond to the cursor. The underestimated become the architecture. Click, tap, or press Space to temporarily accelerate the ascent into a surge.

No build step. Open `index.html` or deploy statically. Uses ES modules + importmap for Three.js r169 + post-processing.
